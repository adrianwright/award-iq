# Limitations

- Award IQ is a synthetic proof of concept, not a production grant-management or eligibility system.
- FO-2026-117 and all institutional, program, personnel, and workflow data are fictional.
- The fixed scenario date is 2026-11-16; external notices and guidance are represented by offline
  synthetic snapshots and are not current agency sources.
- Local M365, email, Teams, reviewer, and task records are fixtures, not live connections. No task,
  email, notification, meeting, approval, funding award, or application submission is created.
- The assessment makes no autonomous funding decision. `Pursue - pending` is a historical human
  disposition with unresolved conditions, not approval.
- Legacy clinical endpoints, fixtures, and optional provisioning/auth scaffolding remain in the
  repository for source-history compatibility. They are not consumed by the Award IQ UI or funding
  route; those legacy live provisioning paths are not an Award IQ deployment recipe.
- No production readiness, compliance, or grant eligibility guarantee is claimed.
