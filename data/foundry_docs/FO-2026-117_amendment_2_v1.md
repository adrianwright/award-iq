# Amendment 2 — Current effective terms

Amendment 2 is the current amendment to FO-2026-117. It supersedes Amendment 1 and raises the
required local match to 25% of total eligible project costs. It also adds a regional-partner
requirement: a signed commitment letter must be included with the application.

The application deadline remains 2026-12-16. The planning basis in this synthetic scenario is
$9,000,000 total eligible project costs; therefore the local-match requirement is $2,250,000.
Prior partner participation is a lead only and does not satisfy the current-letter requirement.
