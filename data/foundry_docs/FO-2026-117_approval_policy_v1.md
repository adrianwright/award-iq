# Funding approval and human review policy

An assessment may provide a grounded recommendation but cannot make the funding decision. The
2026-11-05 review record states “Pursue - pending,” conditional on match confirmation and a partner
commitment letter. Preserve that status until authorized people resolve both conditions and record
a new decision. Review metadata and drafted tasks are not approvals, submissions, or proof of action.
